## Item 2.01. Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets

On February 2, 2026 (the "Closing Date"), Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Northwind" or the "Company") completed its previously announced acquisition of Bluewater Robotics Corp., a Massachusetts corporation ("Bluewater"), pursuant to the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated as of December 18, 2025 (the "Merger Agreement") among the Company, Harbor Merger Sub, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company ("Merger Sub"), Bluewater, and Shareholder Representative Services LLC as representative of Bluewater's securityholders. On the Closing Date, Merger Sub merged with and into Bluewater, with Bluewater surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.

Under the Merger Agreement, the aggregate consideration for all outstanding shares and vested equity awards of Bluewater was approximately $95.0 million, consisting of (i) $70.0 million in cash, subject to customary adjustments for working capital, indebtedness and transaction expenses, and (ii) 1,250,000 shares of the Company's common stock, valued at $25.0 million based on a price of $20.00 per share, the volume-weighted average closing price of the Company's common stock for the twenty (20) trading days ended two (2) trading days before the Closing Date. In addition, Bluewater's former securityholders are eligible to receive contingent consideration of up to $15.0 million in cash if Bluewater's revenue for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026 equals or exceeds $40.0 million, with a proportionate payment for revenue between $32.0 million and $40.0 million and no payment below $32.0 million. Of the cash consideration, $9.5 million was deposited into an escrow account for a period of eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date to secure the indemnification obligations of Bluewater's securityholders.

The Company funded the cash consideration with approximately $30.0 million of cash on hand and a $40.0 million borrowing under its revolving credit facility with First Meridian Bank, N.A., as administrative agent, dated July 15, 2025. Bluewater, headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, develops autonomous mobile robots and fleet management software for warehouse and distribution center operations, and had approximately 210 employees as of the Closing Date. The Company expects Bluewater to contribute approximately $32 million of revenue in fiscal 2026 and to be dilutive to Adjusted EBITDA by approximately $6 million in fiscal 2026 before becoming accretive in fiscal 2027.

The foregoing description of the Merger Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Merger Agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 2.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.

## Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers

Effective on the Closing Date, the Board of Directors of the Company appointed Lena Hoffmann, age 44, as Senior Vice President, Robotics, reporting to the Chief Executive Officer. Ms. Hoffmann founded Bluewater in 2017 and served as its Chief Executive Officer from founding until the Closing Date. Before founding Bluewater, she was Director of Autonomy at Kestrel Automation from 2012 to 2017. In connection with her appointment, Ms. Hoffmann entered into an offer letter with the Company providing for an annual base salary of $360,000, a target annual bonus of 50% of base salary, and a grant of restricted stock units with a grant date value of $2.5 million vesting in equal annual installments over four (4) years, subject to continued employment. Ms. Hoffmann received consideration in the merger in respect of her Bluewater shares on the same terms as other Bluewater securityholders. There are no family relationships between Ms. Hoffmann and any director or executive officer of the Company, and there are no related-party transactions requiring disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.
