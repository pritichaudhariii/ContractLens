# Credit Agreement (Northwind–First Meridian)

This Credit Agreement (the "Agreement") is dated as of July 15, 2025 among Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Borrower"), the lenders party hereto (the "Lenders"), and First Meridian Bank, N.A., as administrative agent, collateral agent, swingline lender and issuing bank (the "Administrative Agent"). This Agreement was filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Borrower's Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

## 1. The Facility

The Lenders severally agree to make revolving loans to the Borrower from time to time during the Availability Period in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding one hundred fifty million dollars ($150,000,000) (the "Revolving Commitments"). The facility includes a sublimit of twenty million dollars ($20,000,000) for letters of credit and a sublimit of ten million dollars ($10,000,000) for swingline loans. Amounts repaid may be reborrowed during the Availability Period. The Borrower may, on up to three (3) occasions, request an increase in the Revolving Commitments in an aggregate amount of up to seventy-five million dollars ($75,000,000) (the "Accordion"), subject to obtaining commitments from existing or new Lenders and pro forma compliance with the financial covenants.

## 2. Maturity

The Revolving Commitments terminate, and all outstanding loans are due and payable, on July 15, 2030 (the "Maturity Date"). Letters of credit may not have an expiration date later than five (5) business days before the Maturity Date unless cash collateralized.

## 3. Interest and Fees

Each loan bears interest, at the Borrower's election, at Term SOFR plus the Applicable Margin or at the Base Rate plus the Applicable Margin less one hundred (100) basis points. Term SOFR is subject to a floor of zero percent (0.00%). The Applicable Margin for Term SOFR loans is determined by the Consolidated Total Net Leverage Ratio as of the most recent quarter end: 1.75% per annum if the ratio is less than or equal to 1.00 to 1.00; 2.00% if greater than 1.00 to 1.00 but less than or equal to 2.00 to 1.00; 2.25% if greater than 2.00 to 1.00 but less than or equal to 3.00 to 1.00; and 2.50% if greater than 3.00 to 1.00. The Borrower will pay a commitment fee on the daily unused portion of the Revolving Commitments at a rate of 0.25% per annum when the ratio is less than or equal to 2.00 to 1.00 and 0.35% per annum otherwise, payable quarterly in arrears. Letter of credit fees equal the Applicable Margin for Term SOFR loans on the daily undrawn amount, plus a fronting fee of 0.125% to the issuing bank. Interest on Term SOFR loans is payable at the end of each interest period of one, three or six months, and interest on Base Rate loans is payable quarterly. Overdue amounts bear interest at the otherwise applicable rate plus 2.00% per annum.

## 4. Guarantees and Security

The obligations are guaranteed by each of the Borrower's material domestic subsidiaries, meaning any domestic subsidiary with total assets or revenues equal to or greater than five percent (5%) of the consolidated total assets or revenues of the Borrower and its subsidiaries. The obligations are secured by a first-priority security interest in substantially all assets of the Borrower and the guarantors, including equity interests in subsidiaries (limited to sixty-five percent (65%) of the voting equity of first-tier foreign subsidiaries), but excluding certain customary excluded assets and any real property with a fair market value below ten million dollars ($10,000,000).

## 5. Financial Covenants

The Borrower will not permit, as of the last day of any fiscal quarter: (a) the Consolidated Total Net Leverage Ratio to exceed 3.50 to 1.00, stepping down to 3.00 to 1.00 for fiscal quarters ending after December 31, 2027, provided that the Borrower may elect to increase the maximum ratio by 0.50 to 1.00 for the four (4) fiscal quarters following a Permitted Acquisition with consideration in excess of fifty million dollars ($50,000,000), on no more than two (2) occasions; (b) the Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio, being Consolidated EBITDA to Consolidated Cash Interest Expense for the trailing four quarters, to be less than 3.00 to 1.00; or (c) Liquidity, being unrestricted cash and cash equivalents plus available Revolving Commitments, to be less than fifty million dollars ($50,000,000). Consolidated EBITDA is calculated with customary add-backs for non-cash stock-based compensation, transaction costs capped at ten percent (10%) of EBITDA, and cost savings from acquisitions realized within eighteen (18) months.

## 6. Negative Covenants

The Borrower and its subsidiaries will not, subject to the exceptions set out in the Agreement: incur indebtedness other than permitted indebtedness, which includes purchase money debt and capital leases up to twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) and a general basket of the greater of thirty million dollars ($30,000,000) and twenty percent (20%) of Consolidated EBITDA; create liens other than permitted liens; make Restricted Payments, except that dividends and share repurchases are permitted without limit so long as no Event of Default exists and the Consolidated Total Net Leverage Ratio is less than 2.00 to 1.00 on a pro forma basis, and otherwise up to fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000) per fiscal year; or make acquisitions, except Permitted Acquisitions, which require no Event of Default, pro forma compliance with the financial covenants, a pro forma Consolidated Total Net Leverage Ratio no greater than 3.00 to 1.00, and aggregate consideration for all Permitted Acquisitions not exceeding one hundred fifty million dollars ($150,000,000) in any fiscal year without the consent of the Required Lenders.

## 7. Mandatory Prepayments

The Borrower will prepay the loans with one hundred percent (100%) of the net cash proceeds of asset sales and casualty events in excess of ten million dollars ($10,000,000) in any fiscal year, to the extent not reinvested in the business within twelve (12) months, and with one hundred percent (100%) of the net cash proceeds of any indebtedness not permitted under this Agreement. Mandatory prepayments reduce the outstanding loans but do not reduce the Revolving Commitments.

## 8. Reporting

The Borrower will deliver to the Administrative Agent: within ninety (90) days after the end of each fiscal year, audited consolidated financial statements with an unqualified opinion from an independent registered public accounting firm of nationally recognized standing; within forty-five (45) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters, unaudited consolidated financial statements; concurrently with each delivery, a compliance certificate signed by a financial officer showing the calculation of the financial covenants; and within sixty (60) days after the start of each fiscal year, an annual budget. Financial statements filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission satisfy the delivery requirement when filed.

## 9. Events of Default

Each of the following is an Event of Default: failure to pay principal when due; failure to pay interest, fees or other amounts within five (5) business days after the due date; any representation or warranty proving materially incorrect when made; breach of the financial covenants or the negative covenants; breach of any other covenant not cured within thirty (30) days after notice; default under other indebtedness with an aggregate principal amount exceeding fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000) that permits acceleration; bankruptcy or insolvency proceedings not dismissed within sixty (60) days; one or more unsatisfied judgments exceeding fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000) for more than sixty (60) days; and a Change of Control, meaning any person or group acquiring more than thirty-five percent (35%) of the Borrower's voting equity or a majority of the board ceasing to be continuing directors. On an Event of Default, the Required Lenders, being Lenders holding more than fifty percent (50%) of the Revolving Commitments, may terminate the commitments and accelerate the loans.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement and the other loan documents are governed by the laws of the State of New York. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the federal and state courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, and waives trial by jury. The Borrower will pay the reasonable fees and expenses of the Administrative Agent's counsel and, on the closing date, an arrangement fee of 0.50% of the Revolving Commitments.
