# Executive Employment Agreement (Northwind–Vasquez)

This Executive Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of September 8, 2025 between Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and Elena Vasquez (the "Executive"). The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on October 1, 2025 (the "Start Date").

## 1. Position and Duties

The Company will employ the Executive as its Chief Financial Officer, reporting to the Chief Executive Officer. The Executive will have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for a chief financial officer of a publicly traded company, including responsibility for financial planning and analysis, accounting, treasury, tax, investor relations and internal controls. The Executive will devote her full business time to the Company, provided that she may serve on one (1) outside board of directors with the prior written consent of the Board, which will not be unreasonably withheld, and may manage personal investments and participate in civic and charitable activities that do not interfere with her duties. The Executive's principal place of work is the Company's headquarters in Austin, Texas.

## 2. Base Salary

The Company will pay the Executive an annual base salary of four hundred twenty-five thousand dollars ($425,000), payable in accordance with the Company's normal payroll practices. The Compensation Committee of the Board will review the base salary at least annually and may increase, but not decrease, it.

## 3. Annual Bonus

For each fiscal year, the Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target equal to sixty percent (60%) of her base salary, based on achievement of Company and individual performance objectives established by the Compensation Committee. The bonus for fiscal year 2025 will be prorated for the portion of the year following the Start Date. Any bonus is paid at the same time bonuses are paid to other senior executives and no later than March 15 of the year following the fiscal year to which it relates, subject to the Executive's continued employment on the payment date except as provided in Section 8.

## 4. Signing Bonus

The Company will pay the Executive a one-time signing bonus of seventy-five thousand dollars ($75,000) within thirty (30) days after the Start Date. If the Executive resigns without Good Reason or is terminated for Cause within twelve (12) months after the Start Date, she will repay the signing bonus in full within thirty (30) days after her separation date.

## 5. Equity Awards

Subject to approval by the Board, the Company will grant the Executive, on the first regularly scheduled grant date after the Start Date, an option to purchase three hundred thousand (300,000) shares of the Company's common stock at an exercise price equal to the fair market value of a share on the grant date. Twenty-five percent (25%) of the option vests on the first anniversary of the Start Date, and the remaining seventy-five percent (75%) vests in thirty-six (36) substantially equal monthly installments thereafter, subject to continued service. The option has a ten (10) year term and is otherwise governed by the Company's 2023 Equity Incentive Plan and the applicable award agreement. The Executive is eligible for annual equity awards beginning in fiscal year 2026 at the discretion of the Compensation Committee.

## 6. Benefits, Vacation and Expenses

The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans made available to senior executives, including medical, dental and vision insurance, life and disability insurance, and the Company's 401(k) plan, under which the Company matches one hundred percent (100%) of contributions up to four percent (4%) of eligible compensation. The Executive is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off per year in addition to Company holidays. The Company will reimburse reasonable business expenses in accordance with its expense policy, and will reimburse documented relocation expenses to the Austin area of up to forty thousand dollars ($40,000), grossed up for taxes.

## 7. Termination of Employment

The Executive's employment is at will. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment at any time with or without Cause, and the Executive may resign at any time with or without Good Reason, provided that the Executive will give the Company at least sixty (60) days' written notice of a resignation without Good Reason. "Cause" means the Executive's willful failure to perform her material duties after written notice and a fifteen (15) day cure period, conviction of or plea of no contest to a felony, willful misconduct causing material harm to the Company, or material breach of this Agreement or the Company's written policies. "Good Reason" means, without the Executive's consent, a material reduction in her base salary or target bonus, a material diminution of her title, duties or authority, a relocation of her principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles, or the Company's material breach of this Agreement, in each case where the Executive gives notice within sixty (60) days after the condition arises, the Company fails to cure within thirty (30) days, and the Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period.

## 8. Severance

If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, in each case other than during the Change in Control Period, the Executive will receive: continued payment of her base salary for twelve (12) months; a prorated annual bonus for the year of termination at target; and payment of COBRA premiums for up to twelve (12) months. If such a termination occurs within the period beginning three (3) months before and ending twelve (12) months after a Change in Control (the "Change in Control Period"), the Executive will instead receive: a lump sum equal to eighteen (18) months of base salary; a lump sum equal to one hundred fifty percent (150%) of her target annual bonus; payment of COBRA premiums for up to eighteen (18) months; and full acceleration of vesting of all outstanding equity awards. All severance is conditioned on the Executive signing and not revoking a general release of claims within sixty (60) days after her separation date and on her continued compliance with Section 9. No severance is payable on termination for Cause, resignation without Good Reason, death or disability, except accrued but unpaid salary, bonus and expenses.

## 9. Restrictive Covenants

During employment and for twelve (12) months after separation, the Executive will not, within the United States, provide services to any business that competes with the Company's supply chain analytics products. During employment and for eighteen (18) months after separation, the Executive will not solicit any employee of the Company to leave its employment or solicit any customer of the Company to reduce its business with the Company. The Executive will hold the Company's confidential information in confidence indefinitely and will comply with the Company's Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement, which is incorporated by reference. The Executive acknowledges that these covenants are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.

## 10. Clawback and Section 409A

All incentive compensation is subject to the Company's Compensation Recovery Policy as in effect from time to time, including as required by the listing standards of the Nasdaq Stock Market. Payments under this Agreement are intended to comply with or be exempt from Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and any payment to a "specified employee" that would be subject to Section 409A will be delayed for six (6) months after separation to the extent required.

## 11. Indemnification

The Company will indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law and the Company's bylaws, and will cover her under its directors' and officers' liability insurance on the same terms as other executive officers, both during and after her employment with respect to her service as an officer.

## 12. Dispute Resolution and Governing Law

Any dispute arising out of this Agreement or the Executive's employment will be resolved by final and binding arbitration administered by JAMS in Austin, Texas under its Employment Arbitration Rules, before a single arbitrator, except that either party may seek injunctive relief in court to enforce Section 9. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 13. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the equity award agreements and the Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes the offer letter dated August 22, 2025. It may be amended only in a writing signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company.
