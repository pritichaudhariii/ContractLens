# Office Lease Agreement (Riverbend–Northwind)

This Office Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is made as of November 20, 2025 between Riverbend Properties LP, a Texas limited partnership ("Landlord"), and Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Tenant").

## 1. Premises

Landlord leases to Tenant the entire fourteenth (14th), fifteenth (15th) and sixteenth (16th) floors of the building located at 1200 Congress Avenue, Austin, Texas 78701 (the "Building"), containing 48,750 rentable square feet in the aggregate (the "Premises"), together with the non-exclusive right to use the Building's common areas. The Building contains 217,600 rentable square feet, and Tenant's Pro Rata Share is 22.4%.

## 2. Term

The term of the Lease is one hundred twenty (120) months, beginning on January 1, 2026 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on December 31, 2035 (the "Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated or extended under this Lease. If Landlord has not delivered the Premises with the Landlord's Work substantially complete by the Commencement Date, the Commencement Date will be delayed day for day, and Tenant will receive two (2) days of free Base Rent for each day of delay beyond January 31, 2026.

## 3. Base Rent

Base Rent for the first Lease Year is thirty-eight dollars and fifty cents ($38.50) per rentable square foot per year, which equals one million eight hundred seventy-six thousand eight hundred seventy-five dollars ($1,876,875) per year, payable in monthly installments of one hundred fifty-six thousand four hundred six dollars and twenty-five cents ($156,406.25) in advance on the first day of each month. Base Rent increases by three percent (3.0%) on each anniversary of the Commencement Date. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Base Rent is fully abated for the first six (6) months of the term (the "Abatement Period"); if Tenant defaults beyond applicable cure periods during the term, the unamortized portion of the abated Base Rent becomes immediately due.

## 4. Operating Expenses and Taxes

Beginning in calendar year 2027, Tenant will pay its Pro Rata Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Real Estate Taxes for each calendar year exceed those for the base year of calendar year 2026 (the "Base Year"). Controllable Operating Expenses (excluding taxes, insurance, utilities and snow removal) may not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a cumulative and compounding basis for purposes of calculating Tenant's share. Landlord will deliver an annual reconciliation statement within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, and Tenant may audit the statement within one (1) year after receipt.

## 5. Security Deposit

On execution of this Lease, Tenant will deliver to Landlord an irrevocable standby letter of credit in the amount of four hundred sixty-eight thousand dollars ($468,000) as security for Tenant's obligations. Provided Tenant has not been in default beyond applicable cure periods, the letter of credit may be reduced to three hundred twelve thousand dollars ($312,000) on the third (3rd) anniversary of the Commencement Date, and to one hundred fifty-six thousand dollars ($156,000) on the sixth (6th) anniversary. Landlord will return the letter of credit within sixty (60) days after the Expiration Date, less any amounts applied to cure a Tenant default.

## 6. Tenant Improvements

Landlord will provide Tenant an allowance of eighty-five dollars ($85) per rentable square foot, for a total of four million one hundred forty-three thousand seven hundred fifty dollars ($4,143,750) (the "TI Allowance"), for the design and construction of Tenant's improvements in accordance with plans approved by Landlord. Any portion of the TI Allowance not used within eighteen (18) months after the Commencement Date is forfeited, except that up to ten dollars ($10) per rentable square foot may be applied to Base Rent. Tenant will pay the cost of improvements exceeding the TI Allowance within thirty (30) days after invoice.

## 7. Parking

Landlord will make available to Tenant one hundred twenty (120) unreserved parking spaces in the Building garage at a monthly charge of one hundred eighty-five dollars ($185) per space, and ten (10) reserved spaces at a monthly charge of three hundred dollars ($300) per space. Parking charges are payable with Base Rent, are abated during the Abatement Period, and may be increased by Landlord no more than once per year to the prevailing rate for comparable buildings in the Austin central business district.

## 8. Use

Tenant will use the Premises only for general office purposes, including a customer briefing center and ancillary uses such as a fitness area and pantry, and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent. Tenant will comply with all applicable laws and the Building rules attached as Exhibit C.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting

Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord may, within thirty (30) days after Tenant's request, elect to recapture any space proposed to be sublet for more than fifty percent (50%) of the remaining term. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on written notice, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an Affiliate of Tenant or to a successor by merger, consolidation or acquisition of substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided the successor has a tangible net worth of at least fifty million dollars ($50,000,000). Fifty percent (50%) of any net profit from a sublease to a third party will be paid to Landlord.

## 10. Renewal Options

Provided no Event of Default exists, Tenant has two (2) consecutive options to extend the term for five (5) years each, on the same terms except that Base Rent for each extension term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent for comparable space in the Austin central business district, determined by the arbitration procedure in Exhibit D if the parties cannot agree. Tenant must exercise each option by written notice given no later than twelve (12) months and no earlier than fifteen (15) months before the then-current Expiration Date.

## 11. Right of First Offer

Before leasing any space on the seventeenth (17th) floor of the Building to a third party, Landlord will first offer the space to Tenant in writing on the terms Landlord intends to offer to third parties. Tenant will have ten (10) business days to accept the offer. If Tenant declines, Landlord may lease the space to a third party on terms not materially more favorable than those offered to Tenant within the following nine (9) months.

## 12. Early Termination Option

Tenant may terminate this Lease effective as of the last day of the eighty-fourth (84th) month of the term by giving Landlord written notice no later than twelve (12) months before that date and paying, together with the notice, a termination fee equal to the sum of the unamortized portion of the TI Allowance, leasing commissions and abated Base Rent, amortized at eight percent (8%) per year, plus four (4) months of the Base Rent and Additional Rent then in effect.

## 13. Default and Remedies

An "Event of Default" occurs if Tenant fails to pay any rent within ten (10) days after written notice that it is past due, or fails to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice (or such longer period as is reasonably required if Tenant is diligently pursuing a cure), or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days. Any rent not paid within five (5) days after its due date bears a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount, and interest at ten percent (10%) per year from the due date until paid. On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate the Lease or Tenant's right of possession and recover damages as permitted by Texas law, subject to Landlord's duty to mitigate.

## 14. Holding Over

If Tenant remains in possession after the Expiration Date without Landlord's written consent, Tenant will be a tenant at sufferance and will pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect for the last month of the term for the first sixty (60) days of holdover, and two hundred percent (200%) thereafter, together with any consequential damages suffered by Landlord after such sixty (60) day period.

## 15. Insurance

Tenant will maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least three million dollars ($3,000,000) per occurrence and five million dollars ($5,000,000) in the aggregate, property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and improvements, and workers' compensation as required by law, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds on the liability policy. Landlord will maintain property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost.

## 16. Governing Law and Notices

This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Notices must be in writing and delivered by nationally recognized overnight courier or certified mail to the addresses in the signature block, with a copy by email, and are effective on receipt or refusal.
