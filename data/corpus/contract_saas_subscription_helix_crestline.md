# SaaS Subscription Agreement (Helix–Crestline)

This SaaS Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of September 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Helix CRM, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 233 South Wacker Drive, Chicago, Illinois 60606 ("Helix"), and Crestline Bank, N.A., a national banking association with its principal office at 1 Crestline Plaza, Denver, Colorado 80202 ("Customer"). Helix will provide Customer with access to the Helix Enterprise customer relationship management platform (the "Service") on the terms below.

## 1. Subscription and Order Form

Customer subscribes to Helix Enterprise for one thousand two hundred (1,200) named users at a subscription fee of three hundred sixty dollars ($360) per named user per year, for an annual subscription fee of four hundred thirty-two thousand dollars ($432,000), as set out in Order Form No. HX-2025-0917 (the "Order Form"). The subscription includes the Sales, Service and Marketing modules, the Helix API at the Enterprise rate limit of 10,000 calls per minute, and Premier Support. Customer may add named users at any time; users added mid-term are billed at thirty dollars ($30) per user per month, prorated for the remainder of the then-current subscription year.

## 2. Term and Renewal

The initial subscription term is twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term"). The subscription automatically renews for successive twelve (12) month terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term. Helix may increase the per-user subscription fee for a Renewal Term by no more than five percent (5%) over the fee in effect for the preceding term, provided that Helix gives notice of the increase at least ninety (90) days before the Renewal Term begins.

## 3. Fees and Payment

The annual subscription fee is invoiced annually in advance and is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Overage and add-on fees are invoiced monthly in arrears. Late amounts accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month. Fees are non-cancellable and non-refundable except as expressly provided in Sections 8 and 10. All fees are stated and payable in United States dollars and exclude applicable taxes, which Customer will pay unless it provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 4. Service Availability

Helix will make the Service available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95%, measured for each calendar month and excluding Scheduled Maintenance. "Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance performed during the standing window of Sundays between 2:00 a.m. and 6:00 a.m. Eastern Time, not exceeding eight (8) hours in any calendar month, and announced through the Helix Trust Portal at least five (5) business days in advance. Emergency maintenance required to address a security vulnerability is not counted as unavailability if it does not exceed two (2) hours per month. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage is below 99.95%, Customer is entitled to a service credit of ten percent (10%) of the monthly-equivalent subscription fee; below 99.5%, twenty-five percent (25%); and below 99.0%, fifty percent (50%). Credits must be claimed within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month and are Customer's sole remedy for unavailability.

## 5. Support

Premier Support is available 24x7 by telephone and portal. Priority 1 incidents (Service unavailable for all Customer users, or a security incident) receive an initial response within thirty (30) minutes and a resolution target of four (4) hours. Priority 2 incidents (major functionality degraded for many users) receive an initial response within two (2) hours and a resolution target of one (1) business day. Priority 3 incidents (minor functionality impaired or how-to questions) receive an initial response by the next business day. Helix assigns Customer a named Technical Account Manager who conducts quarterly service reviews.

## 6. Customer Data, Hosting and Security

Customer Data is hosted in Helix's production environments in the United States, in the AWS us-east-1 (Northern Virginia) and us-west-2 (Oregon) regions, and will not be transferred outside the United States without Customer's written consent. Customer Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Helix performs full backups daily and retains backups for thirty-five (35) days. Helix maintains SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II reports, renewed annually, and engages an independent firm to perform penetration testing of the Service at least once per year; summaries of the reports are available to Customer under this Agreement's confidentiality terms. Customer may export Customer Data at any time in CSV or JSON format through the Service or on request.

## 7. Subprocessors and Audits

Helix maintains a list of subprocessors on the Helix Trust Portal and will give Customer at least thirty (30) days' written notice before authorizing any new subprocessor to process Customer Data. Customer may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days of the notice; if the parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the affected portion of the Service and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees. Customer or its regulator may audit Helix's compliance with this Agreement once in any twelve (12) month period, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, during normal business hours, subject to Helix's reasonable security policies; additional audits are permitted following a Security Incident or as required by a banking regulator with supervisory authority over Customer.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice. If Customer terminates for Helix's uncured breach, Helix will refund the prepaid subscription fees attributable to the remainder of the then-current term. Helix may suspend Customer's access on ten (10) days' notice for undisputed fees more than sixty (60) days past due. After termination or expiration, Helix will make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days, after which Helix will delete Customer Data from its production systems within sixty (60) days and from backups in the ordinary course.

## 9. Regulatory Compliance

Helix acknowledges that Customer is subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and regulations of the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, and agrees to maintain safeguards for Customer Data consistent with the Interagency Guidelines Establishing Information Security Standards. Helix will cooperate with examinations by Customer's regulators, provide reasonable access to relevant records, and notify Customer of any regulatory inquiry that specifically concerns Customer Data. Helix will provide Customer with an annual business continuity summary, including a recovery time objective of four (4) hours and a recovery point objective of one (1) hour for the Service.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Except for either party's indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, or Helix's breach of Section 6, each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement will not exceed the greater of five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000) or the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Helix's aggregate liability for a Security Incident caused by its breach of Section 6 will not exceed two (2) times the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, except in connection with the excluded claims listed above.

## 11. Insurance

Helix will maintain, at its own expense, cyber and privacy liability insurance with limits of not less than five million dollars ($5,000,000) per claim and technology errors and omissions insurance with limits of not less than five million dollars ($5,000,000) per claim, and will provide certificates of insurance to Customer annually.

## 12. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Any dispute will be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois, and each party waives trial by jury. Before filing suit, the parties will escalate the dispute to their respective executive sponsors for a period of at least thirty (30) days.
