# Master Services Agreement (Northwind–Acme)

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 1200 Congress Avenue, Austin, Texas 78701 ("Provider"), and Acme Logistics Holdings, LLC, a Nevada limited liability company with offices at 4400 Harbor Way, Reno, Nevada 89501 ("Customer"). Provider and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## 1. Definitions

"Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Customer Data" means all data, records and content uploaded to the Services by or on behalf of Customer. "Documentation" means Provider's then-current user guides for the Services. "Order" means an ordering document or Statement of Work executed by both Parties under this Agreement. "Security Incident" means any confirmed unauthorized access to, or acquisition, disclosure or loss of, Customer Data in Provider's possession. "Services" means the Northwind Analytics platform and any related professional services described in an Order.

## 2. Services and Statements of Work

Provider will perform the Services described in each Order in accordance with the Documentation and this Agreement. Each Order will identify the Services, fees, deliverables, milestones and any acceptance criteria. In the event of a conflict between an Order and this Agreement, the Agreement controls unless the Order expressly states that it overrides a specific section of this Agreement. Provider may use subcontractors but remains responsible for their performance.

## 3. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter it automatically renews for successive twelve (12) month renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Terms are together the "Term." Orders that are outstanding at expiration continue to be governed by this Agreement until completed.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Customer will pay the fees stated in each Order. Unless an Order states otherwise, Provider invoices subscription fees monthly in arrears and professional services fees upon milestone acceptance. All invoices are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. Customer must notify Provider in writing of any disputed invoice amount within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, and the Parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Provider may increase subscription fees at the start of each Renewal Term by no more than the lesser of four percent (4%) or the increase in the Consumer Price Index for the preceding twelve months, on sixty (60) days' notice. Fees are exclusive of taxes; Customer is responsible for all sales, use and value-added taxes other than taxes on Provider's income.

## 5. Service Levels and Service Credits

Provider will make the platform available at least 99.9% of the minutes in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least five (5) business days in advance and not exceeding six (6) hours per month ("Monthly Uptime"). If Monthly Uptime falls below the commitment, Customer is entitled to a credit against the next invoice as follows: Monthly Uptime of at least 99.0% but below 99.9% earns a credit of five percent (5%) of the monthly subscription fee; Monthly Uptime of at least 95.0% but below 99.0% earns a credit of ten percent (10%); and Monthly Uptime below 95.0% earns a credit of twenty-five percent (25%). Credits are capped at twenty-five percent (25%) of the monthly subscription fee for the affected month, must be requested in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month, and are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for unavailability. If Monthly Uptime falls below 95.0% in any three (3) months during a rolling twelve-month period, Customer may terminate the affected Order without penalty.

## 6. Support

Provider will provide support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for Severity 1 issues (production platform unavailable or a material feature unusable with no workaround) with an initial response within one (1) hour. Severity 2 issues (material feature impaired with a workaround) receive an initial response within four (4) hours during business hours, and Severity 3 issues (all other issues) receive an initial response within one (1) business day. Provider will provide status updates for Severity 1 issues at least every two (2) hours until resolution.

## 7. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach. Either Party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed. After the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term, Customer may terminate any subscription Order for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, provided that Customer pays a termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the subscription fees that would otherwise have been payable for the remainder of the then-current term. Upon any termination, Provider will provide up to ninety (90) days of transition assistance at its then-current professional services rates, and Customer will pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Sections 4, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15 and 16 survive termination.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each Party will protect the other Party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. Confidential Information excludes information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction. The obligations in this Section continue for five (5) years after termination of this Agreement, except that obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 9. Data Protection and Security

Provider will maintain an information security program that includes administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Customer Data, and will maintain SOC 2 Type II attestation, renewed annually, and provide the report to Customer on request. Provider will notify Customer of any Security Incident without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after confirming the Security Incident, and will provide reasonable cooperation with Customer's investigation and any required notifications. Provider will process Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions. Within thirty (30) days after termination, Provider will make Customer Data available for export in a commonly used, machine-readable format, and will delete Customer Data from its production systems within ninety (90) days after termination, except for copies retained in routine backups, which are deleted in the ordinary course within one hundred eighty (180) days.

## 10. Intellectual Property

Provider retains all right, title and interest in the Services, the Documentation and any improvements, including any suggestions or feedback provided by Customer. Customer retains all right, title and interest in Customer Data and grants Provider a limited license to process Customer Data solely to provide the Services. Upon full payment, Provider grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any deliverables identified in an Order for Customer's internal business purposes.

## 11. Warranties

Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation and that professional services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance of the non-conforming Services, or if Provider cannot re-perform within thirty (30) days, a refund of the fees paid for the non-conforming Services. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the Services are provided "as is" and Provider disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement.

## 12. Indemnification

Provider will defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services, as provided by Provider, infringe a patent, copyright or trademark or misappropriate a trade secret, and will pay damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement. Provider has no obligation for claims arising from Customer Data, modifications not made by Provider, or combinations with products not supplied by Provider. Customer will defend Provider against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in violation of law. The indemnified Party must give prompt written notice, sole control of the defense to the indemnifying Party, and reasonable cooperation.

## 13. Limitation of Liability

Except for the Excluded Claims, each Party's total cumulative liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. "Excluded Claims" means a Party's breach of Section 8 (Confidentiality), a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 12, and a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Provider's total liability for Security Incidents caused by Provider's breach of Section 9 will not exceed two (2) times the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, up to a maximum of five million dollars ($5,000,000). Neither Party is liable for lost profits or for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, even if advised of their possibility, except with respect to the Excluded Claims.

## 14. Insurance

During the Term, Provider will maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least two million dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence, cyber liability insurance of at least ten million dollars ($10,000,000) per claim and in the aggregate, technology errors and omissions insurance of at least five million dollars ($5,000,000) per claim, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Provider will furnish certificates of insurance on request and will give Customer thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 15. Non-Solicitation

During the Term and for twelve (12) months after its termination, neither Party will directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was materially involved in performing or receiving the Services, provided that general advertisements not targeted at such employees do not violate this Section.

## 16. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any dispute arising out of this Agreement, and each Party waives any right to a jury trial. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

## 17. Miscellaneous

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign it without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of all or substantially all of its assets. Notices must be in writing and delivered by courier or email with confirmation to the addresses in the signature block. Neither Party is liable for failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that if such an event continues for more than thirty (30) days the other Party may terminate the affected Order. This Agreement, together with all Orders, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties.
