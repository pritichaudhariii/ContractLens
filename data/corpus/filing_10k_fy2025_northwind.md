## Item 1. Business

Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Northwind," the "Company," "we" or "our") provides a cloud-based analytics platform for logistics and supply chain operations. The platform ingests shipment, inventory, telematics and order data from customers' systems and provides forecasting, exception management and network optimization tools. We were incorporated in Delaware in 2014 and completed our initial public offering in April 2023. Our headquarters are in Austin, Texas. We report two segments: Platform Subscriptions, which represented 84% of revenue in fiscal 2025, and Professional Services, which represented 16%. As of December 31, 2025 we served 3,240 customers, including 412 customers with annual recurring revenue above $100,000, and our net revenue retention rate was 117%. As of December 31, 2025 we had 1,860 full-time employees, of whom 780 were in research and development, 610 in sales and marketing, 290 in customer success and professional services, and 180 in general and administrative functions. None of our employees are represented by a labor union.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to numerous risks, including the following. Customer concentration: our largest customer, Acme Logistics Holdings, LLC, accounted for approximately 11% of our revenue in fiscal 2025, and our ten largest customers together accounted for approximately 34% of revenue; the loss of, or a significant reduction in purchases by, any of these customers could materially reduce our revenue. Reliance on third-party infrastructure: our platform is hosted on Amazon Web Services, and any disruption of that service would impair our ability to deliver the platform. Cybersecurity: we store large volumes of customers' operational data, and a security breach could result in liability, loss of customers and reputational harm. Competition: we compete with larger enterprise software vendors that have greater resources, as well as with in-house solutions. Acquisition integration: our acquisition of Bluewater Robotics Corp. exposes us to hardware supply chains, warranty obligations and integration risks that differ from our software business. Seasonality and macroeconomic conditions in the freight industry could reduce demand for our products. We have a history of net losses and may not sustain profitability on a GAAP basis.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings

On August 14, 2025, Datamere Corp. filed a complaint against us in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas alleging that our route optimization features infringe two of its patents and seeking damages of approximately $18 million and injunctive relief. We believe the claims are without merit and intend to defend the action vigorously. We have not recorded a liability for this matter because we do not believe a loss is probable. From time to time we are also involved in other legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business, none of which we believe to be material.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue for fiscal 2025 was $412.6 million, an increase of 27% from $324.9 million in fiscal 2024. Platform Subscriptions revenue was $346.6 million, an increase of 30%, and Professional Services revenue was $66.0 million, an increase of 14%. The increase in subscription revenue was driven by the addition of 540 net new customers and expansion within existing customers, as reflected in our net revenue retention rate of 117%. Gross margin was 74% in fiscal 2025 compared to 71% in fiscal 2024, reflecting improved hosting efficiency and a higher subscription mix. Operating expenses were $336.5 million, including research and development of $128.4 million, sales and marketing of $152.7 million and general and administrative of $55.4 million. Loss from operations was $31.2 million compared to $58.7 million in fiscal 2024. Net loss was $28.4 million, or $0.39 per share, compared to a net loss of $52.1 million, or $0.74 per share, in fiscal 2024. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was $14.8 million, our first full year of positive Adjusted EBITDA, compared to negative $19.6 million in fiscal 2024. Free cash flow was $22.1 million compared to negative $11.3 million in fiscal 2024. Remaining performance obligations were $621 million as of December 31, 2025, of which approximately 62% is expected to be recognized as revenue over the next twelve months. As of December 31, 2025 we had cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $310.4 million and no borrowings outstanding under our $150 million revolving credit facility. For fiscal 2026 we expect revenue of $505 million to $515 million, representing growth of 23% to 25%, including approximately $32 million from Bluewater Robotics, and Adjusted EBITDA of $38 million to $44 million.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk

Our exposure to interest rate risk relates primarily to our revolving credit facility, which bears interest at a floating rate based on Term SOFR; a hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would increase annual interest expense by approximately $0.4 million for each $40 million drawn. Approximately 12% of our revenue in fiscal 2025 was denominated in currencies other than the U.S. dollar, principally the euro, the British pound and the Canadian dollar. We do not currently hedge our foreign currency exposure.
