# Data Processing Addendum (Helix–Crestline)

This Data Processing Addendum (the "DPA") forms part of the SaaS Subscription Agreement dated September 15, 2025 (the "Agreement") between Helix CRM, Inc. ("Helix") and Crestline Bank, N.A. ("Customer"), and applies to the extent Helix processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer in providing the Service. Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Roles of the Parties

For the purposes of Data Protection Law, Customer is the controller of Personal Data processed through the Service and Helix is the processor acting on Customer's behalf. Where Customer acts as a processor for its own clients, Helix acts as a sub-processor, and Customer warrants that its instructions are authorized by the relevant controller. "Data Protection Law" means all laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.

## 2. Details of Processing

The subject matter of the processing is the provision of the Service under the Agreement. The duration is the term of the Agreement plus the deletion period in Section 9. The nature and purpose are the storage, retrieval, analysis and display of customer relationship data as instructed by Customer through the Service. The categories of data subjects are Customer's employees and contractors who use the Service, and Customer's clients, prospective clients and their representatives. The categories of Personal Data are names, business and personal contact details, job titles, account identifiers, interaction history, and transaction metadata such as product holdings and case notes. No special categories of Personal Data and no full payment card numbers are to be submitted to the Service.

## 3. Processor Obligations

Helix will process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, including the Agreement, this DPA and Customer's configuration of the Service, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law, in which case Helix will inform Customer of that requirement before processing unless the law prohibits such notice. Helix will immediately inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law. Helix will ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive data-protection training at least annually.

## 4. Security Measures

Helix will implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Annex 2, which include encryption of Personal Data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, multi-factor authentication for all administrative access, role-based access controls reviewed quarterly, centralized logging with security logs retained for twelve (12) months, annual independent penetration testing, background checks for personnel with access to production systems, and an annual SOC 2 Type II examination. Helix may update the measures from time to time provided the updates do not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

## 5. Personal Data Breach

Helix will notify Customer of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer's Personal Data without undue delay and in any event no later than forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of it. The notification will describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects, and will identify a contact point for further information. Helix will provide updates as further information becomes available and will not notify data subjects or regulators of a breach on Customer's behalf unless Customer instructs it to do so or the law requires it.

## 6. Sub-processors

Customer generally authorizes Helix to engage sub-processors to process Personal Data, subject to the conditions in this Section. The current list of sub-processors is published on the Helix Trust Portal. Helix will give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor, and Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days after the notice. Helix will impose on each sub-processor data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA and remains fully liable to Customer for the performance of each sub-processor's obligations.

## 7. Data Subject Requests and Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Helix will assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights, and will forward to Customer, within five (5) business days, any such request that Helix receives directly. Helix will provide the assistance required to respond to a data subject request within ten (10) business days of Customer's written request. Helix will also provide reasonable assistance with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities, at Customer's expense where the assistance requires material effort beyond the standard features of the Service.

## 8. International Transfers

Personal Data is hosted in the United States as described in the Agreement. To the extent Personal Data subject to the GDPR or UK GDPR is transferred to Helix in the United States, the parties enter into the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two, controller to processor) adopted by the European Commission decision of 4 June 2021, together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the UK Information Commissioner, each of which is incorporated by reference. For transfers from Switzerland, the Standard Contractual Clauses apply with the modifications required by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

## 9. Deletion and Return

Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, and following the thirty (30) day export period described in the Agreement, Helix will delete all Personal Data from its production systems, and will delete or return all remaining Personal Data, within ninety (90) days after the end of the Services, unless applicable law requires continued storage, in which case Helix will continue to protect the Personal Data under this DPA and will not process it further. Helix will certify deletion in writing on Customer's request.

## 10. Audits

Helix will make available to Customer all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including its most recent SOC 2 Type II report and penetration test summary. Customer may conduct an on-site audit once in any twelve (12) month period on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, or at any time following a Personal Data Breach or where required by a supervisory authority. Audits will be conducted during normal business hours, will not unreasonably interfere with Helix's operations, and will be at Customer's expense unless the audit reveals a material non-compliance with this DPA, in which case Helix will bear the reasonable costs of the audit.

## 11. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability set out in the Agreement, except that nothing in this DPA limits liability that cannot be limited under Data Protection Law. This DPA is governed by the same law as the Agreement, except that the Standard Contractual Clauses are governed by the law of Ireland as required by their terms.

## Annex 1: Processing Details

Controller: Crestline Bank, N.A. Processor: Helix CRM, Inc. Contact for data protection matters at Helix: privacy@helixcrm.example. Frequency of transfer: continuous for the term of the Agreement. Retention: term of the Agreement plus ninety (90) days. Competent supervisory authority for EU transfers: the Irish Data Protection Commission.

## Annex 2: Technical and Organizational Measures

Access control: unique accounts, multi-factor authentication for administrative and production access, quarterly access reviews, least-privilege role assignments. Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2 or higher in transit, keys managed in a hardware-backed key management service with annual rotation. Resilience: daily backups retained for thirty-five (35) days, multi-region replication, recovery time objective of four (4) hours. Monitoring: centralized security logging retained for twelve (12) months, 24x7 security operations monitoring. Assurance: annual SOC 2 Type II examination, annual independent penetration test, vulnerability scanning at least weekly with critical findings remediated within seven (7) days. Personnel: background checks before granting production access, confidentiality agreements, annual security and privacy training.
